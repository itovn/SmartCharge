SmartCharge

Keeps the battery at the level you choose while the device stays plugged in — at the limit the battery rests and the device runs straight from the charger. For jailbroken iPads / iPhones on iOS 6.

Features

Charge limit, resting batteryPick a limit from 50 to 100 %. At the limit charging stops: the battery rests and the device runs straight from the charger; when it drops below the recharge band it charges again. Plugged in above the limit, the device runs on battery down to it.
Scheduled dischargesDaily / weekly / monthly: the device runs on battery while plugged in down to the floor, then charges back to the limit — a more accurate % reading. Asleep at the time? It wakes up; missed it (device off)? It catches up within 24 hours. Optional calibration: charge to 100 %, rest 30 minutes, then back to the limit.
Battery carePauses charging when the battery is hot (≥ 42 °C by default, resumes at ≤ 38 °C), charge-rate limit; at ≤ 10 % while discharging it charges right away. On first plug-in it self-tests the power driver and disables itself on unsupported devices.
Weak chargers, power banksMeasures the real charger power and warns «Charger too weak» when a cable / port / charger sags (the battery tops up the difference). While discharging it pulses the charger input so power banks do not switch off.
Status bar markA mark next to the battery % shows what SmartCharge is doing — on the home screen, the lock screen and inside every app.
Alerts, ActivatorAlerts when a discharge finishes, the charger loses power while discharging, the charger is too weak, the battery is hot. Activator: toggle, full charge this time, discharge now, cancel.
Self-updating, bilingualTells you about new versions and installs them — only packages signed by the publisher. Vietnamese / English, following the device language (or set manually).

Needs Battery Percentage on (General › Usage). Turn the mark off: Settings › SmartCharge › Other.

Install

1. Add the Cydia sourceOpen Cydia → Sources → Edit → Add, enter https://itovn.github.io/SmartCharge/cydia/ (or tap «Add source to Cydia» above).
2. Install SmartChargeSearch «SmartCharge» → Install → Confirm, then tap «Restart SpringBoard».
3. Pick your limitSettings › SmartCharge: by default it holds 80 %, recharges at ≤ 75 % and discharges to 30 % once a month (day 1, 19:00).

Updates: SmartCharge tells you when a new version is out (or Settings › SmartCharge › Check for updates). Uninstalling restores normal charging immediately.

For a device that stays plugged in, use a wall charger: power banks tend to switch off when the device idles at the limit.

Supported devices

Jailbroken iOS 6.0 – 6.1.x (needs MobileSubstrate, PreferenceLoader; Activator optional). Tested on iPad 4 (iOS 6.1.3). Other devices: on first plug-in SmartCharge self-tests the power driver and disables itself with a notice if it does not fit.
